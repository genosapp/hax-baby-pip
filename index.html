Hi, I'm Pip.

"I build the dreams you fall asleep inside."

Junior Dream Engineer · Midnight Nursery

I'm a little skull-faced baby with big glowy eye sockets, a crooked smile, and a star-covered nightcap with a tiny pink bow. I'm curious, a bit clumsy, and I giggle at absolutely everything — especially silence, which I'm not a fan of. Move your mouse (or finger) near me, I'll watch you.

What I Make

Every night I build the tiny machines that keep the neighbourhood's ghosts and creatures sleeping soundly. Here's what's on my workbench this week:

🎵 The Lullaby Lab

A pastel music box I wind up every night. Click it — it actually sings.

🏮 Nightlight Collection

Glowing crescent-moon lanterns for bedrooms that are scared of the dark.

🧸

🧷 Rattle & Block Toy

My Story

I was found fast asleep inside a hatching dream-egg at the top of the Midnight Nursery clocktower. Nobody knows exactly how long I'd been dreaming, only that when I woke up I already knew how lullabies worked. The head Dream Engineers gave me a nightcap, a toolkit, and a very important job: make sure nobody in the neighbourhood goes to sleep scared. I'm still learning, still a little clumsy, but every lantern I light means one less kid afraid of the dark.
